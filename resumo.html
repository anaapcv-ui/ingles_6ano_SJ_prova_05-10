✅ Verb To Be — affirmative (am / is / are)

p.91 · falamos de pessoas e coisas: sentimentos, profissões, objetos, lugares e nacionalidades

SujeitoTo beForma curtaExemplo
IamI'mI am a climate activist.
He / She / Itishe's / she's / it'sHe is aware of the dangers.
You / We / Theyareyou're / we're / they'reThey are installing a warning system.
It is dangerous to go outside during bad weather.
We are part of the eco-team at school.
Cuidado: sujeito no plural pede are → "Floods and droughts are effects of climate change." E é sempre I am (nunca "I is" nem "I are").

❓ Verb To Be — questions & short answers

p.91 · para perguntar, o verbo vem ANTES do sujeito

Pergunta (To Be question)Yes 👍No 👎
Are you an activist, too? Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
Is he aware of the dangers? Yes, he is.No, he isn't.
Is it dangerous to go outside? Yes, it is.No, it isn't.
Are they installing a warning system? Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
Regra de ouro: a resposta curta afirmativa NÃO contrai → "Yes, I am." (nunca "Yes, I'm"). A negativa pode contrair → "No, he isn't."

🚫 Verb To Be — negative forms

p.94 · acrescentamos "not" ao to be quando algo não é verdade

SujeitoForma completaForma curta
II am notI'm not
He / She / ItHe is notisn't / he's not
You / We / TheyWe are notaren't / we're not
She isn't a singer. She isn't a sports star.
The glaciers aren't safe.
Atenção: com "I" a forma curta é I'm not (não existe "amn't"). E isn't = is not · aren't = are not.
